About Us

A trusted manpower partner for electrical and plumbing projects across Saudi Arabia.

Our Story

Abdulrahman Attia Subhi Al-Atawi Trading & Contracting Group was founded to bridge the gap between projects that need skilled manpower and workers seeking opportunity. Based in Jeddah, we specialize in supplying trained electrical and plumbing workers and technicians to contractors and companies across Saudi Arabia.

Over the years we have built a reputation for reliability, speed and professionalism, helping clients meet deadlines without compromising on quality.

Mission

To supply skilled, trained and dependable manpower that empowers projects to succeed.

Vision

To be the leading manpower supply partner for electrical and plumbing trades in the Kingdom.
